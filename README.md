# AskFrankie for Mac

AskFrankie for Mac is the Mac app for Frankie, the CRM for any business or team with an AI built in. This repository holds the app's public downloads and the update feed (`appcast.xml`) the app checks for new versions. The app's source lives in the main AskFrankie repository.

## Facts

- **What it does:** the Mac app for Frankie, the CRM with an AI built in. It also lets Frankie work in Mac apps, Messages and notifications on your Mac.
- **Who it is for:** anyone with an AskFrankie account (web app: https://app.askfrankie.com.au).
- **Cost:** the download is free. You need an AskFrankie account and a trial or plan. Prices are on https://askfrankie.com.au/pricing/.
- **Needs:** macOS 14 or later. One download for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Latest version:** 0.2.58 (see `appcast.xml`).
- **Where it lives:** the ZIP is on this repo's [Releases](https://github.com/ImLukeF/askfrankie-mac-releases/releases/latest) page. The app updates itself from `appcast.xml`.

Download the latest ZIP from [Releases](https://github.com/ImLukeF/askfrankie-mac-releases/releases/latest); unzip it, then drag AskFrankie to Applications. Sign in with your AskFrankie account and the Mac connects automatically. Native connection, Messages, voice and permission controls live in AskFrankie Settings. Updates arrive automatically in-app.
